What we do

The full project lifecycle — advise, review, commission, assure.

An independent expertise layer that big rental houses and EPC contractors don't provide: we protect the owner's interest from first drawing to final handover.

S/01

Owner's engineer

We sit on your side of the table — managing scope, design and vendors so the delivered plant matches what you paid for.

S/02

Commissioning & handover

Structured commissioning, punch and snag control, testing and handover documentation — so start-up is predictable, not a scramble.

S/03

Electrical safety & named responsibility

Named responsible-person duties as a service, plus safety cases and compliance to ESA, SS-EN 50110, FSE and SFS 6002.

S/04

Design review & studies

Independent review of electrical design and contracts — earthing, protection, sizing and constructability — before mistakes are built in.

S/05

Alarm rationalisation (ISA-18.2)

Audit and rationalise control-system alarms against ISA-18.2 to cut alarm floods and operator overload.

S/06

Regulatory & compliance

Documentation and liaison for Tukes, SSM and DSB — the approval-gating paperwork, handled properly.
